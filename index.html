Claim Check

Misinformation is read, not searched for. Paste any claim and get the evidence for and against it, with sources.

Try one:

How it works

  1. You paste a claim. Anything you saw online, in a group chat, on the news.
  2. Claim Check pulls the key words out of it and searches Wikipedia for the articles that cover the topic.
  3. Each article is read and sorted: does it line up with the claim, push back on it, or just give background? Phrases like “myth”, “debunked” and “no evidence” count as pushback.
  4. You get a verdict and the sources. The verdict is a guide, the linked articles are the real answer. Read them before you share the claim.

No account, no tracking, no API key. Everything runs in your browser and talks only to Wikipedia's public API. This first version checks Wikipedia only; more sources are coming.
